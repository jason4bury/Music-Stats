    Help

    Music Stats reads the tags of the music in your folders and shows your collection in numbers. It only reads: it never changes, moves or tags a file.

    Looking closer

    • Click any bar or name (a decade, year, genre, artist, album, format or letter) to see the stats for just that. Each one you pick shows above the numbers; press its × to drop it, or Clear all.
    • Pick a collection at the top to see one of your music folders on its own.
    • If your music is in two places (such as a PC and a NAS), the first list at the top picks which copy to show. They're never added together, so nothing is counted twice.
    • Type in the search box to narrow everything to an artist, album, song or genre with those words in it.
    • Top artists can be ranked by tracks, playing time, albums or the disk space they take.
    • In Genres by decade, click a square to see that genre in that decade. In Artists A to Z, click a letter for the artists under it ("The Beatles" are under B).

    What the numbers mean

    • Years, genres, artists and albums come from each file's tags. A track with no year or genre isn't counted in those charts; Missing tags shows how many; press Show files on a line to see them (or Show folders for artists with no picture, with where each artist.jpg would go).
    • Albums are counted by album artist and album name, so a "Greatest Hits" by two artists counts twice.
    • A track has cover art if the file has a picture in it, or its folder has one such as cover.jpg or folder.jpg.
    • Album covers come from a picture beside the music (cover.jpg, folder.jpg, front.jpg and the like) or, failing that, the picture inside the files. Click an album to see its cover and numbers at the top.
    • Artist pictures come from an artist.jpg (or .png) in the artist's folder. Click an artist to see their picture and numbers at the top.
    • Recently added and Added to your collection use each file's date.
    • Track lengths sorts every track into bands from under 2 minutes to over 10, with the shortest tracks below.

    Choosing the music folders

    Open Settings and press Add folder. Click through the folders to the one you want, give it a name (such as Music or Soundtracks) and press Add this folder. Remove takes a folder off; its tracks drop out of the numbers after the rescan that starts straight away. If the same music is in two places (such as a PC and a NAS), give each folder a library, such as PC or NAS; the page then shows one library at a time.

    Rescanning

    New and changed files are read when the app starts, every 24 hours, and when you press Rescan. Files that haven't changed aren't read again, so a rescan is quick. If a music folder can't be found (an unplugged drive or a sleeping NAS), its tracks from the last scan are kept. While it scans, a bar under the top bar shows how far it has got, and the time left shows beside Rescan.
